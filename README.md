## The Problem

**WAN 2.2 prompt engineering is complex and intimidating.** Most users struggle with:

- **Optimal prompt structure** - WAN 2.2 responds best to 80-120 word prompts with specific formatting
- **Cinematic terminology** - Camera movements, lighting setups, and shot types require professional knowledge
- **Style consistency** - Achieving cohesive aesthetic requires understanding of film techniques
- **Technical parameters** - Balancing quality settings, motion blur, and rendering options
- **Prompt optimization** - Knowing which elements WAN 2.2's MoE architecture prioritizes

Result: **Amateur prompts = amateur videos**, even with WAN 2.2's powerful capabilities.

## The Solution

**prompt-weaver** eliminates the complexity. Just describe your idea and let the tool handle the professional cinematic enhancement.

**Simple Input:**
```

"Muscular superhero in confident stance, cape billowing, defined physique"

```

**Enhanced WAN 2.2 Prompt:**
```

"Muscular superhero in confident stance, cape billowing, defined physique, medium shot, dolly in, flying, harsh lighting, 4k resolution, dynamic angles, dramatic lighting, high energy, intense atmosphere, cinematic composition, professional videography, high quality, detailed, masterpiece, WAN 2.2 optimized"

```

## 🚀 Features

### **🎥 Cinematic Lab**
- **Interactive Dropdowns** for shot opening, camera movement, motion, style, lighting, and technical parameters
- **Enhanced Camera Movements** with directional specificity:
  - Dolly in/out, Pan left/right, Tilt up/down
  - Tracking forward/backward, Orbit left/right, Crane up/down
- **Professional Style Integration** automatically applied

### **✨ 8 Professional Style Packs**
- **🎬 Film Noir** - Dramatic shadows, high contrast, venetian blind lighting
- **🌆 Cyberpunk** - Neon lighting, rain-soaked streets, digital atmosphere  
- **🌅 Golden Hour** - Warm golden tones, film grain, classic cinematography
- **🧚 Fantasy** - Soft focus, magical lighting, surreal atmosphere
- **💥 Action** - Dynamic angles, dramatic lighting, high energy
- **🌿 Natural** - Golden hour lighting, natural colors, peaceful mood
- **👻 Horror** - Eerie shadows, cold lighting, ominous atmosphere
- **💡 Studio** - Bright lighting, clean composition, professional

### **⚙️ Advanced Features**
- **Style Weaver** - Professional cinematography controls for advanced users
- **Motion Craft** - Realistic motion and physics simulation settings
- **Pro Controls** - Enhanced realism parameters (18+ age verification required)
- **Realism Engine** - Maximum detail and hyperrealistic rendering options

### **🛡️ Responsible Design**
- **Age verification system** for mature content controls
- **Single-file deployment** - No installation required, runs in any browser
- **Offline capable** - Works without internet connection
- **Cross-platform** - Compatible with Windows, Mac, Linux, mobile

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Download and open** `prompt-weaver.html` in your browser
2. **Enter your subject** in the Custom Subject field (e.g., "beautiful landscape at sunset")
3. **Configure dropdowns** for your desired cinematic style
4. **Select a style pack** by clicking on one of the 8 professional options
5. **Click "Generate Cinematic Prompt"** to create your enhanced prompt
6. **Copy the result** and paste into your WAN 2.2 video generation tool

**That's it!** Your simple idea is now a professional, WAN 2.2-optimized cinematic prompt.
